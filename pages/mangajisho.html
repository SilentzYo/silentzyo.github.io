Manga Jisho

Projects GitHub

Manga Jisho is an OCR-based Japanese translation pipeline built with Python, Tesseract OCR, and OpenCV. It extracts Japanese text from images with 95% accuracy and translates it while preserving meaning, about 15% faster than alternatives.
