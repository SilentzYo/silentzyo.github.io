Manga Jisho

Projects Resume GitHub

Manga Jisho is a Chrome extension and local Python server for reading Japanese manga in the browser. It detects manga pages on any site, reads their text with mokuro and manga-ocr, and works a couple of pages ahead to allow continuous reading once started. Holding Shift and hovering over a word shows its JMdict definitions in a side panel. Text is split into words with fugashi, then neighbouring words are joined and conjugated forms are matched to their dictionary form; ex. 食べなかった still finds 食べる.

Page translation replaces the Japanese with English right on the page. OpenCV erases the original text, using a flat fill on plain bubbles. A flood fill traces each speech bubble, DeepL translates every bubble with the rest of the page as context, and the English is sized and wrapped to fit inside the bubble's shape. A quantized version of the OCR model trades a little accuracy for speed on slower machines.
